Board development programmes · Ethiopia

Better boards, built on lessons already learned.

Executive education for Ethiopian boards, chairs, board secretaries and directors.

Sankofa Consulting Limited is a board governance advisory, training and consultancy firm working from Addis Ababa and Nairobi. We help Ethiopian and East African boards become more effective, commercially astute, accountable and risk-conscious, and able to create long-term value.

Our courses respond to real boardroom decisions. Leave with practical tools, board templates and decisions you can apply at your next meeting.

What the fees include

All fees are per participant and exclude applicable taxes.

Addis Ababa open courses: Tuition, course materials, lunch and refreshments at a four-star hotel.

Kenya residential programmes in Nairobi or Mombasa: Tuition, materials, full-board single-room accommodation for one night more than the training days, and a sightseeing day. International flights and airport transfers are not included.

In-house programmes: Programmes for a single board are quoted separately. Durations are indicative and can be adjusted to your board’s needs.
