The moment

Ethiopia's boards are entering a new era.

State corporations are being opened to new owners through the Ethiopian Securities Exchange. Boards that once answered only to the state must now meet the standards of listed companies: sharper oversight, fuller disclosure and greater accountability to shareholders.

Kenya went through this change during its own privatisation and listing waves. We were in its boardrooms and its governance institutions. Sankofa exists to carry those lessons across, so Ethiopia can move faster and avoid mistakes others have already made.

About Sankofa

An ancient idea for a modern boardroom.

Sankofa is an ancient concept and Adinkra symbol of the Akan people of Ghana. In the Twi language, san means to return and kofa means to fetch or take. Together they mean "go back and get it." The philosophy is simple: it is not wrong to go back for the knowledge, wisdom and heritage of the past in order to build a better future.

The symbol is often drawn as a bird with its feet planted firmly forward and its head turned back to take a precious egg from its back. The forward feet stand for progress. The backward glance stands for memory and respect for roots. The egg stands for future generations and the knowledge carried forward to them.

We see every well governed board in that bird. It learns from what has gone before, moves confidently forward, and protects what it will pass on.
Our story

Built on a transition we have lived through.

Over two decades, Kenya built the codes, institutions and board practices needed to govern privatised and listed companies. The journey was not always smooth, and much was learned along the way.

Ethiopia now stands at a similar point, with the added advantage of being able to learn from others. Sankofa was founded to make that possible: an equal partnership of Ethiopian and Kenyan governance practitioners, combining local knowledge and relationships with proven experience of what works.

Directors body

A professional home for Ethiopia's directors.

Many countries have a national institute of directors: a professional body that sets standards, develops directors and speaks for good governance. Ethiopia does not yet have one. As its corporations move to new ownership, the need has never been greater.

Why it matters

  • It sets professional standards and a code of conduct for directors
  • It offers continuing development and recognised qualifications
  • It connects directors across sectors to share experience
  • It gives directors a trusted voice in shaping governance policy

Our role

Sankofa's partners bring direct experience of building and leading such an institution. Our Kenyan partners served on the Board of the Institute of Directors Kenya, one as its Chairman. Duncan Watta also sits on an Africa Corporate Governance Network committee mandated to help establish governance institutions across the continent. We are working with respected Ethiopian directors to form a founding group and draft a founding charter.

How it will come together

  1. Founding group Respected Ethiopian directors come together to lead the initiative.
  2. Charter The founding group agrees the body's purpose, membership, governance and code of conduct.
  3. Registration The body is formally registered in Ethiopia.
  4. Launch Membership opens to directors across Ethiopia.

Why does Kenya's experience matter for Ethiopia?

Over two decades, Kenya built the codes, institutions and board practices needed to govern privatised and listed companies. Our Kenyan partners were in its boardrooms and led its institute of directors. Combined with our Ethiopian partner's local knowledge, that helps Ethiopian boards move faster and avoid mistakes others have already made.

What is the directors membership body?

A national professional body for Ethiopia's directors that sets standards, offers continuing development and speaks for good governance. Sankofa is supporting respected Ethiopian directors to found it. Serving directors can register their interest today.

What does the name Sankofa mean?

Sankofa is an Akan word from Ghana meaning "go back and get it." It teaches that there is wisdom in returning to what has been learned in order to build a better future.
